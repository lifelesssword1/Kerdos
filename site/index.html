Questions

Is the report really free?

Yes. No card and no access needed. We send one per business, and you decide whether you want us to continue.

Do I need to share passwords?

No. You add us as a user on Google Ads and as a manager on your Google Business Profile, by invitation. You can remove us any time.

When do I pay?

Only if you decide to go ahead, after you've seen your free report. The Growth Plan is €150 a month, or €250 with the Content package. No contract, cancel with one click.

What exactly is guaranteed?

That your first monthly report shows both money saved on ads and a measurable improvement (reviews, Maps calls or website visits). If either is missing, we refund your first month in full.

What if I don't run Google Ads?

Everything else still applies. The guarantee is then based on improvements to your profile and website.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No one can honestly promise that. We guarantee the work gets done every month, and we show you the results.

Can you make ChatGPT recommend my business?

No one can guarantee what an AI assistant says. What we can do is fix what these assistants rely on: your website explaining clearly what you do and where, the same correct details across Google, Bing, maps and directories, recent reviews, and letting their search tools read your site. Each month we check whether you show up and report what changed.

Who does the work?

Kerdos is a service of SGD Ventures. We use our own AI-assisted analysis tools under human oversight, and every change is listed in your monthly report.
