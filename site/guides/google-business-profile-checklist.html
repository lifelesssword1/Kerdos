Google Business Profile checklist for local businesses

Your profile is often the first thing a customer sees. These ten checks take about an hour and cost nothing.

The checklist

Work through these in order.

  1. Business name exactly as it appears on your shopfront or van, with no added keywords.
  2. Primary category that matches your main service, plus relevant secondary categories.
  3. Phone number and website correct, and the same as on your own site.
  4. Opening hours correct, including special hours for bank holidays and holidays.
  5. Service area or address set correctly. If you travel to customers, list the areas you really serve.
  6. Services listed, each with a short description in plain words.
  7. At least ten real photos of your work, team and premises, and replace old ones once a year.
  8. A post every week or two: a recent job, an offer, a seasonal reminder.
  9. Questions and answers: add the five questions customers ask most, with clear answers.
  10. Every review answered, politely, within a day or two.

What to watch

Do not buy or invent reviews, and do not stuff the name with keywords. Google can suspend profiles for both, and a suspended profile can mean no calls until it is reinstated.

Questions

How often should I update my profile?

Post every week or two and review the checklist once a month.

Do I need a website to have a profile?

No, but a clear website helps customers and helps search engines and AI assistants understand the business.
